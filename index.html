About the Model
This model has been tested against real UFC fights going back to 2015 — over a thousand fights across every weight class. It only flags a bet when it finds a meaningful gap between what the odds imply and what the data suggests. Most fights get passed on entirely.

The backtest is run in a way that simulates real conditions: the model never looks ahead, and is only trained on fights that already happened before the card being evaluated. What you see in the numbers is as close to a real-world track record as a model can produce.

The Past Year tab shows live results as they come in. Once a fight is more than a year old it rolls off automatically. Until enough real results accumulate, those numbers are drawn from the historical backtest.

What the Model Looks At
The model evaluates each fighter based on their complete UFC history — how dominant they have been, how their fighting style matches up against their opponent, and how they have been performing recently. Physical attributes and career trajectory also factor in, but the biggest signal is always how much better or worse a fighter has looked than the market expects.
